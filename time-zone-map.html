Frequently Asked Questions

How does the interactive time zone map work?

The map shows the current day and night regions across the globe based on the sun's position. Cities are marked with dots that show the current local time. Click any city dot to see detailed time information including UTC offset and DST status.

How many cities are shown on the time zone map?

Over 600 major cities worldwide are displayed on the interactive map, covering all 24 time zones and all populated continents.

Does the map update in real time?

Yes, the day/night overlay and city times update every second to reflect the current time globally. The terminator line (day/night boundary) moves continuously.

Can I search for a specific city on the map?

Yes — use the search box at the top of the map. Type any city name and it will center the map on that city and show its current time.

What do the colors on the map mean?

Yellow = Day (6 AM–6 PM local), Dark blue = Night (6 PM–6 AM local), Orange = Dawn/Dusk transition zones. Blue dots = cities (click for current time).
