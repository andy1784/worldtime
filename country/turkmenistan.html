🇹🇲 Time in Turkmenistan

Turkmenistan uses Turkmenistan Time (TMT), which is UTC+5:00. The country does not observe daylight saving time. Turkmenistan is a Central Asian country known for its vast natural gas reserves and its capital Ashgabat, which holds the world record for the highest density of white marble-clad buildings. The country is largely covered by the Karakum Desert. Ashgabat, the capital, is the largest city and the political and economic center of the country. Turkmenistan's time zone aligns it with neighboring Uzbekistan and western Kazakhstan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time zone is Turkmenistan in?

Turkmenistan uses TMT (UTC+5) year-round with no daylight saving time.

Does Turkmenistan observe daylight saving time?

No, Turkmenistan has never observed daylight saving time since independence.

Time in Daily Life

Turkmenistan uses UTC+5 year-round with no daylight saving, so Ashgabat, Turkmenabat, and Dasoguz all share one clock.

The country has kept a fixed offset for years, which simplifies rail and business timing across its desert and oasis cities.

Summer heat pushes activity into the early morning and late evening, with the cooler night hours being the most comfortable for being outdoors.

The Tejen-Merv rail and the desert road network connect Ashgabat to the east on one UTC+5 clock, and the country's compact size keeps travel times short.
