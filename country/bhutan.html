🇧🇹 Time in Bhutan

Bhutan uses Bhutan Time (BTT), which is UTC+6:00. The country does not observe daylight saving time. Bhutan is a small Himalayan kingdom sandwiched between India (UTC+5:30) and China (which uses UTC+8 nationwide despite spanning five geographical zones). Bhutan's choice of UTC+6 places it between these two neighbors. Thimphu, the capital, is the only capital city in the world without traffic lights. Bhutan measures national success through "Gross National Happiness" rather than GDP, and the country has maintained a careful balance between modernization and cultural preservation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time zone is Bhutan in?

Bhutan uses BTT (UTC+6) year-round with no daylight saving time.

Does Bhutan share a time zone with any neighbor?

No — India uses UTC+5:30, China uses UTC+8. Bhutan's UTC+6 is unique in the region.

Time in Daily Life

Bhutan's UTC+6 places it neatly between its giant neighbours, and Thimphu, Phuntsholing, and Punakha all share one national clock with no daylight saving.

The country once planned to shift to UTC+7 or UTC+8 to better align with regional trading partners, but the idea was dropped, and Bhutan has kept its traditional timing.

Bhutanese life follows the Buddhist calendar as much as the clock, with many festivals set by lunar dates rather than fixed Gregorian hours, so 'on time' can mean something different from town to town.
