🇿🇦 Time Zones in South Africa

South Africa uses a single time zone (SAST, UTC+2) across the entire country. South Africa has never observed DST. The country's position at UTC+2 means it has good overlap with both European and Asian business hours — a 6-hour difference with London and a 7-hour difference with Beijing.
