Time in Daily Life

Kenya uses East Africa Time, UTC+3, year-round with no daylight saving, so Nairobi, Mombasa, and Kisumu all share one clock.

The workday typically runs 08:00 to 17:00, and the country's equatorial position keeps sunrise and sunset steady near 06:30 and 18:30 throughout the year.

Evening life centres on family and food, with dinners after 19:00 and the cooler hours after sunset being the most pleasant for being outdoors.

The safari high season from July to October draws visitors to the Maasai Mara, and the coast's drier months from December to March are the most popular for beach travel.

The Standard Gauge Railway links Nairobi to Mombasa, and mobile-money and bus networks keep the cities moving on one shared clock from dawn to past 21:00.
