🇦🇫 Time in Afghanistan

Afghanistan uses Afghanistan Time (AFT), which is UTC+4:30. It's one of only a few countries worldwide with a half-hour time zone offset. The offset reflects the country's position between Iran (UTC+3:30) and Pakistan (UTC+5:00). Afghanistan does not observe daylight saving time. Kabul, the capital and largest city, sits at an elevation of about 1,800 meters in a narrow valley between Hindu Kush mountains. The country's time zone has remained unchanged for decades despite significant political upheaval.
