🇵🇹 Time Zones in Portugal

Portugal uses Western European Time (WET, UTC+0) — the same as the UK. It's the westernmost country in mainland Europe. Lisbon has become a growing tech hub, hosting the annual Web Summit conference. Portugal's time zone aligns it with London, making it convenient for UK business.

Time in Daily Life

Portugal uses Western European Time with EU daylight saving, so Lisbon and Porto shift with London rather than with Madrid, despite being on the Iberian peninsula.

The workday often runs 09:00 to 18:00 with a long lunch, and summer evenings see dinners after 20:00 and the riverside cafes busy past 22:00.

Madeira and the Azores keep their own earlier zones, so a call from Lisbon to the Azores crosses an hour even within one country.

The summer festival season from June fills Lisbon and Porto with street parties, and August see many shops close as families head to the Algarve or the coast.
