🇨🇴 Time Zones in Colombia

Colombia uses Colombia Time (COT, UTC-5) year-round. The country briefly observed DST in 1992-93 but no longer does. Bogotá is the capital, sitting at 2,640 meters above sea level. Colombia's economy has been growing rapidly, and Medellin has transformed from a troubled city into a model of urban innovation.
