🇵🇭 Time Zones in Philippines

The Philippines uses Philippine Time (PHT, UTC+8) year-round. Manila is one of the most densely populated cities in the world, and the country is a major hub for business process outsourcing (BPO). The Philippines' time zone is the same as China, Singapore, and Taiwan.
