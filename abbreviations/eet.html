Frequently asked questions about EET

What does EET stand for?

EET stands for Eastern European Time, UTC+2, observed in Greece, Finland, Ukraine, the Baltic states and several others.

What is EEST?

Eastern European Summer Time, UTC+3 — the daylight-saving variant used from late March to late October.

What is the EET to CET difference?

One hour. Athens is always one hour ahead of Rome or Berlin.
