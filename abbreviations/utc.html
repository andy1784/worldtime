UTC is not a time zone you can live in — it is the reference every other offset is measured against. The odd abbreviation comes from a compromise between English (CUT) and French (TUC): both sides got the same letters.

UTC quick facts

  • Full name: Coordinated Universal Time
  • Standard offset: UTC±0
  • Daylight saving: none
  • Where it applies: Global standard: aviation, computing, GPS, weather services; no local population lives on UTC proper

Frequently asked questions about UTC

What does UTC stand for?

UTC stands for Coordinated Universal Time (in English) or Temps Universel Coordonné (in French). The mixed abbreviation satisfies both languages.

Is UTC a time zone?

Not exactly. It is the global reference standard; local zones are expressed as offsets like UTC+3 or UTC−7.

Why do servers log in UTC?

Because UTC never shifts for daylight saving, server timestamps stay unambiguous across seasons and countries.
