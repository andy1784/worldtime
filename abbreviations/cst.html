CST has a rare double meaning: in Asia the same letters stand for China Standard Time (UTC+8). Context matters — a meeting "at 10 CST" means two different moments depending on which side of the Pacific you are on.

CST quick facts

  • Full name: Central Standard Time
  • Standard offset: UTC−6
  • Daylight saving: CDT (UTC−5), March–November
  • Where it applies: US Midwest and South (Chicago, Dallas, Houston, New Orleans), Mexico (as Tiempo del Centro), Central America

Frequently asked questions about CST

What does CST stand for?

In North America CST stands for Central Standard Time (UTC−6). The same abbreviation is also used for China Standard Time (UTC+8), which causes frequent confusion.

Is Chicago always on CST?

No. From March to November Chicago follows CDT (UTC−5) because of daylight saving.

How far is CST from EST?

One hour behind. When it is noon EST it is 11 AM CST.
