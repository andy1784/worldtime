India stretches almost 30 degrees of longitude yet uses a single offset chosen as a compromise between Mumbai and Kolkata. Note that IST also means Irish Standard Time and Israel Standard Time — another abbreviation collision.

Frequently asked questions about IST

What does IST stand for?

Most commonly India Standard Time, UTC+5:30. The same abbreviation also refers to Irish Standard Time and Israel Standard Time, so context matters.

Why is India 5:30 hours off UTC?

The half-hour offset splits the difference between the country’s eastern and western extremes, keeping sunrise times reasonable nationwide.

Does India have daylight saving?

No. India has stayed on IST year-round since 1962.
