Arizona is the famous exception: it stays on MST all year without daylight saving, so in summer Arizona shares a clock with Pacific daylight time while Denver moves ahead.

MST quick facts

  • Full name: Mountain Standard Time
  • Standard offset: UTC−7
  • Daylight saving: MDT (UTC−6), March–November (except Arizona)
  • Where it applies: US Mountain states (Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake City), Alberta, parts of Idaho and Montana

Frequently asked questions about MST

What does MST stand for?

MST stands for Mountain Standard Time, UTC−7, used in the Rocky Mountain region of North America.

Does Arizona use MST year-round?

Yes. Most of Arizona does not observe daylight saving and stays on MST (UTC−7) all year.

What is the MST to EST conversion?

EST is 2 hours ahead of MST.
