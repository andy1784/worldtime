PST governs Silicon Valley and Hollywood, so it became the de-facto clock of the tech industry. Server logs, release notes and "PT" timestamps in software usually mean this zone.

PST quick facts

  • Full name: Pacific Standard Time
  • Standard offset: UTC−8
  • Daylight saving: PDT (UTC−7), March–November
  • Where it applies: US West Coast (Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle), British Columbia, Nevada

Frequently asked questions about PST

What does PST stand for?

PST stands for Pacific Standard Time, UTC−8, observed on the US West Coast and western Canada during winter months.

What is PT vs PST?

PT (Pacific Time) is the umbrella label. In winter it equals PST (UTC−8); in summer it becomes PDT (UTC−7).

What is the difference between PST and EST?

EST is 3 hours ahead of PST. When it is 9 AM in Los Angeles it is already noon in New York.
