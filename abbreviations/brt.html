Brazil spans four offsets, but BRT carries the country’s business rhythm. Brazil scrapped national daylight saving in 2019 after studies showed negligible savings — one of the largest recent countries to do so.

BRT quick facts

Frequently asked questions about BRT

What does BRT stand for?

BRT stands for Brasília Time, UTC−3, the main Brazilian offset used by São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Brasília.

Does Brazil have daylight saving now?

No. Brazil abolished daylight saving nationally in 2019.

How many time zones does Brazil span?

Four, from UTC−2 (Atlantic islands) to UTC−5 (western Acre).
