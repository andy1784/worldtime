Frequently asked questions about KST

What does KST stand for?

KST stands for Korea Standard Time, UTC+9. Both Korean states effectively share this offset today.

Did North Korea ever change its time zone?

Yes — it introduced UTC+8:30 in 2015 and reverted to UTC+9 in May 2018.

Is KST the same as JST?

Yes, both are UTC+9, so Seoul and Tokyo always share the same wall-clock time.
