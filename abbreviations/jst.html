Japan tried daylight saving during the US occupation (1948–1951) and dropped it. JST has been fixed at UTC+9 ever since, which keeps scheduling with Tokyo pleasantly predictable.

JST quick facts

  • Standard offset: UTC+9

Frequently asked questions about JST

What does JST stand for?

JST stands for Japan Standard Time, UTC+9. Japan does not observe daylight saving.

What is the JST to PST difference?

JST is 17 hours ahead of PST in winter and 16 hours ahead of PDT in summer.

Has Japan ever used DST?

Briefly, during the American occupation between 1948 and 1951. It was abolished afterwards and never returned.
