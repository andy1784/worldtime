Hawaii skipped daylight saving partly because day length barely changes near the tropics — sunrise varies by under an hour and a half across the whole year, making DST pointless there.

HST quick facts

  • Full name: Hawaii Standard Time
  • Standard offset: UTC−10
  • Daylight saving: none
  • Where it applies: Hawaii year-round; also Tahiti and Cook Islands (as separate zone names)

Frequently asked questions about HST

What does HST stand for?

HST stands for Hawaii Standard Time, UTC−10. Hawaii does not observe daylight saving.

What is the HST to EST difference?

Five hours. When it is 9 AM in New York it is 4 AM in Honolulu.

Does Hawaii ever change clocks?

Never — Hawaii and most of Arizona are the only US states that skip daylight saving entirely.
