CET covers around 500 million people, more than any other single UTC+1 adoption. Spain is geographically aligned with GMT but politically sits in CET — a legacy decision attributed to Franco-era alignment with Berlin.

CET quick facts

  • Full name: Central European Time
  • Standard offset: UTC+1
  • Daylight saving: CEST (UTC+2), late March–late October
  • Where it applies: Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Sweden, Norway

Frequently asked questions about CET

What does CET stand for?

CET stands for Central European Time, UTC+1, used by most of continental Europe including Germany, France, Italy and Spain.

What is CEST?

Central European Summer Time — the same zone at UTC+2 during daylight-saving months, from late March to late October.

What is the CET to GMT difference?

CET is one hour ahead of GMT in winter, and CEST is two hours ahead of GMT in summer.
