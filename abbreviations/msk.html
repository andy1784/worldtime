Russia spans 11 time zones, but MSK is the political anchor: federal schedules, TV broadcasting and even railway tickets are published against Moscow Time regardless of where you physically are.

MSK quick facts

  • Full name: Moscow Time
  • Standard offset: UTC+3
  • Daylight saving: none

Frequently asked questions about MSK

What does MSK stand for?

MSK stands for Moscow Time, UTC+3, the reference zone used across European Russia, Belarus and much of the former USSR infrastructure.

Does Russia use daylight saving?

No. Russia cancelled permanent seasonal clock changes in 2011 and settled on fixed offsets per zone.

How many zones does Russia have?

Eleven, from Kaliningrad (UTC+2) to Kamchatka (UTC+12).
