About Daylight Saving Time in 2026

Daylight Saving Time (DST) is observed in over 70 countries worldwide. In 2026, most of North America and Europe will adjust their clocks twice a year. The US and Canada "spring forward" on March 8, 2026 and "fall back" on November 1, 2026. European countries change on March 29 and October 25, 2026.

Not all countries observe DST. Most of Asia (including China, Japan, India) and Africa do not change clocks. Some countries like Iceland, Turkey, and most of Russia have permanently abolished DST.
