How We Ensure Accuracy

Time zone data is surprisingly complex. Countries change their DST rules, new time zones are created, and political decisions can shift a region's UTC offset overnight. Here's how we stay accurate:

  • IANA Time Zone Database: We utilise-t-il the industry-standard IANA database (also known as the Olson database), which is maintained by volunteers and updated multiple times per year.
  • Real-time calculation: All times are calculated client-side using your device's clock, ensuring sub-second accuracy.
  • Automatic DST handling: Daylight saving transitions are handled automatically — you never need to manually adjust.
  • Regular updates: Our city database is reviewed and updated regularly to reflect geopolitical changes.
