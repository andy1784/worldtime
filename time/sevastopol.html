About Sevastopol

Sevastopol is a port city on the Black Sea, historically known as the home of Russia's Black Sea Fleet. The city has been under Russian occupation since 2014, though Ukraine and most of the international community consider it Ukrainian territory.

Time Zone Details

  • IANA Timezone: Europe/Kyiv
  • Standard time: EET (UTC+2)
  • Summer time: EEST (UTC+3)
  • DST schedule: Last Sunday March → Last Sunday October

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is it in Sevastopol right now?

Does Sevastopol observe daylight saving time?

Yes. Clocks go forward 1 hour on the last Sunday in March (to EEST, UTC+3) and back 1 hour on the last Sunday in October (to EET, UTC+2).

What is the time difference between Sevastopol and New York?

Sevastopol is 7 hours ahead of New York (EST) in winter and 6 hours ahead during the DST overlap period (March–October).
