About Sydney

Sydney is Australia's largest city and financial capital. The Sydney Opera House and Harbour Bridge are iconic, but the city's real economic power comes from finance, tech, and tourism. Sydney observes DST, switching to AEDT (UTC+11) in summer.

Time Zone Details
