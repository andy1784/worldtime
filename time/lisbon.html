About Lisbon

Lisbon is Portugal's capital and the westernmost capital in mainland Europe. Portugal is in the same time zone as the UK (WET/GMT in winter, WEST in summer). The city's maritime history shaped global timekeeping — Portuguese navigators were among the first to need accurate time for navigation.
