About Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires is Argentina's capital and the 'Paris of South America.' Argentina abolished DST in 2009 and stays on ART (UTC-3). The city's European-influenced architecture and tango culture make it unique in Latin America.

Time Zone Details

  • IANA Timezone: America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires
  • Daylight Saving: No, Buenos Aires does not observe daylight saving time. The UTC offset stays the same throughout the year.
