Frequently Asked Questions

What is a world clock?

A world clock displays the current local time for multiple cities around the globe simultaneously. It uses the IANA time zone database to automatically account for daylight saving time (DST) and UTC offsets.

How many cities does this world clock cover?

This world clock covers 695+ major cities worldwide across all 24 time zones and 67+ countries, including every capital city and major metropolitan area.

Does the world clock automatically adjust for daylight saving time?

Yes — it uses the IANA time zone database which includes all historical and current DST rules. The clock updates automatically when DST transitions occur in any region.

Can I embed this world clock on my website?

Yes! We provide a free embeddable world clock widget at /embed with iframe and JavaScript embed options for WordPress, HTML, React, Notion, and other platforms.

How accurate is the time shown?

The time is synchronized to UTC and uses your browser's local time as reference. It's accurate to the second and updates in real-time.

What time zones are supported?

All 24 standard time zones plus half-hour (UTC+5:30, UTC+9:30) and quarter-hour (UTC+5:45, UTC+12:45) offsets are fully supported.
