Best Time to Call China from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

China uses a single time zone (CST, UTC+8) despite spanning 5 geographical time zones. This simplifies calling but the gap with the West is still significant.

China Time Zone Facts

  • CST (China Standard Time): UTC+8, no DST
  • China is 8 hours ahead of GMT/UTC
  • China is 13 hours ahead of US Eastern (EST)
  • China is 16 hours ahead of US Pacific (PST)

Best Times to Call China from the US

  • 6-8 PM EST = 7-9 AM CST next day (perfect for Beijing business hours)
  • 5-7 PM PST = 9-11 AM CST next day (good for late morning China)

Popular Chinese cities: Beijing | Shanghai | Guangzhou | Shenzhen | Chengdu

Best Times to Call China from Europe

  • 9-11 AM London = 5-7 PM CST (good for end of China business day)
  • 10 AM - 12 PM CET = 5-7 PM CST

China Business Hours & Holidays

  • Standard business hours: 9 AM - 6 PM CST
  • Lunch break: 12-1 PM (many offices close)
  • Golden Week holidays: Chinese New Year (Jan/Feb), National Day (Oct 1-7)
  • Avoid calling during Golden Week — most businesses closed
