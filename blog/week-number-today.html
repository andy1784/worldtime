The ISO 8601 system numbers weeks Monday-through-Sunday; week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of January. Europe uses it widely for planning, payroll and manufacturing schedules; North America less so, which causes confusion in international teams.

Leap Years

A year is a leap year if it divides by 4, except centuries — those must divide by 400. So 2000 was a leap year but 1900 wasn't, and 2100 won't be. The next leap years after 2024 are 2028, 2032, 2036. Leap years shift weekday-based calendars like ISO weeks by a day after February.
