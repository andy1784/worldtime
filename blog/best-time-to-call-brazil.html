Best Time to Call Brazil from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

Brazil spans 4 time zones, but most business activity is in BRT (UTC-3). Here's how to call Brazil from anywhere.

Brazil Time Zones

  • BRT (Brasilia Time): UTC-3 (main business zone)
  • AMT (Amazon Time): UTC-4
  • ACT (Acre Time): UTC-5
  • Brazil no longer observes DST (abolished 2019)

Best Times to Call Brazil from the US

  • 9 AM - 12 PM EST = 10 AM - 1 PM BRT (excellent overlap)
  • 1-5 PM EST = 2-6 PM BRT (perfect for business calls)

Popular Brazilian cities: Sao Paulo | Rio de Janeiro | Brasilia | Salvador

Best Times to Call Brazil from Europe

  • 2-5 PM London = 11 AM - 2 PM BRT (good overlap)
  • 3-6 PM CET = 11 AM - 2 PM BRT

Brazil Business Culture

  • Business hours: 9 AM - 6 PM BRT
  • Lunch: 12-2 PM (longer than many countries)
  • Carnival (February/March): Many businesses closed
  • Brazilians are relationship-focused — small talk is important
