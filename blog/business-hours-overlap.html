The practical overlap between two offices is where both local clocks sit inside roughly 9 AM–5 PM. For distant pairs that window can be one hour or none at all — which is when you either move meetings to someone's evening or switch part of the work to async updates.

Best Time to Call London

London works 9 AM–5 PM GMT/BST. That means:

  • From New York: 9 AM–12 PM your time hits their afternoon.
  • From San Francisco: 9 AM–11:30 AM Pacific overlaps London's late day.
  • From Sydney: only early-morning Sydney (~6–8 AM) reaches London's afternoon.
