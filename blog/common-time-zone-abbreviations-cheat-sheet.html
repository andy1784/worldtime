Why Time Zone Abbreviations Are Confusing

The core problem: the same abbreviation can mean different things in different regions. CST = Central Standard Time (US, UTC-6), China Standard Time (UTC+8), or Cuba Standard Time (UTC-5). IST = Indian Standard Time (UTC+5:30), Irish Standard Time (UTC+1), or Israel Standard Time (UTC+2).

Pro Tip: Use UTC Offsets

When scheduling international calls, always use UTC offsets instead of abbreviations. Saying "UTC+5:30" is unambiguous; saying "IST" could mean India, Ireland, or Israel. Our meeting planner shows UTC offsets for all cities.
