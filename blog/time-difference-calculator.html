How to Calculate Time Differences

  1. Find each city's UTC offset — For example, New York is UTC-5 (EST) and Tokyo is UTC+9 (JST)
  2. Subtract the offsets — 9 - (-5) = 14 hours difference
  3. Check for DST — During summer, New York becomes UTC-4, making it 13 hours
  4. Use our tool — We do all this automatically at worldtimessync.com

The Trickiest Time Differences

Some time differences are confusing because of DST transitions that happen at different dates in different countries:

  • New York ↔ London: Usually 5h, but can be 4h or 6h during DST transition weeks in March and October
  • New York ↔ Sydney: Usually 15-16h, but flips because US and Australia have opposite DST seasons
  • London ↔ New York: The "5 hours ahead" rule changes to 4h for about 2 weeks per year
  • India ↔ anywhere: India (UTC+5:30) has a half-hour offset, making calculations less intuitive

Time Differences for Business

If you work with international teams, here are the best overlapping business hours:

  • New York ↔ London: 9-11 AM EST = 2-4 PM GMT (3 hour overlap)
  • New York ↔ India: 7-9 AM EST = 5:30-7:30 PM IST (limited overlap)
  • London ↔ Singapore: 8-10 AM GMT = 4-6 PM SGT (2 hour overlap)
  • San Francisco ↔ Tokyo: 5-7 PM PST = 9-11 AM JST next day
