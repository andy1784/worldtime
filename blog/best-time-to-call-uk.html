Best Time to Call the UK from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

The UK uses GMT (UTC+0) in winter and BST (UTC+1) in summer. This guide helps you find the perfect time to call from anywhere in the world.

UK Time Zone Basics

  • GMT (Greenwich Mean Time): UTC+0 (late October to late March)
  • BST (British Summer Time): UTC+1 (late March to late October)
  • UK business hours: 9 AM - 5 PM local time

Best Times to Call the UK from the US

  • 8-10 AM EST = 1-3 PM UK (ideal overlap)
  • 12-2 PM EST = 5-7 PM UK (good for after-work calls)
  • Avoid: Before 7 AM EST (too early for UK)

Popular UK cities: London | Manchester | Edinburgh | Birmingham

Best Times to Call the UK from Asia

  • 8-10 PM Tokyo = 11 AM-1 PM UK (winter) / 10 AM-12 PM UK (summer)
  • 7-9 PM Beijing = 11 AM-1 PM UK (winter)

Best Times to Call the UK from Australia

  • 8-10 PM Sydney = 9-11 AM UK (winter) / 10 AM-12 PM UK (summer)
  • Best window: Late evening Australia = Morning UK

UK Business Hours & Holidays

Standard UK business hours are 9 AM - 5:30 PM. Banks close at 4:30 PM. Avoid calling on Bank Holidays (New Year's, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Early May, Spring, Summer, Christmas, Boxing Day).
