Best Time to Call the UAE from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

The UAE uses GST (Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4) year-round. With Dubai and Abu Dhabi as major business hubs, knowing when to call is crucial.

UAE Time Zone

  • GST (Gulf Standard Time): UTC+4, no DST
  • UAE is 4 hours ahead of GMT/UTC
  • UAE is 9 hours ahead of US Eastern (EST)
  • UAE is 12 hours ahead of US Pacific (PST)

Best Times to Call the UAE from the US

  • 7-9 AM EST = 4-6 PM GST (good for end of UAE business day)
  • 9-11 AM EST = 6-8 PM GST (after-hours for UAE)

Popular UAE cities: Dubai | Abu Dhabi

Best Times to Call the UAE from Europe

  • 9 AM - 12 PM London = 1-4 PM GST (perfect overlap)
  • 10 AM - 1 PM CET = 2-5 PM GST

Best Times to Call the UAE from Asia

  • 1-3 PM Tokyo = 8-10 AM GST (good for morning UAE)
  • 12-2 PM Beijing = 8-10 AM GST

UAE Business Hours & Culture

  • Government: 7:30 AM - 2:30 PM (Sun-Thu)
  • Private sector: 9 AM - 6 PM (some 8 AM - 5 PM)
  • Work week: Sunday - Thursday (Fri-Sat weekend)
  • Ramadan: Reduced business hours
  • Major holidays: Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, UAE National Day (Dec 2)
