How Many Time Zones Are There? — By Country & Worldwide

���� August 10, 2026  ·  ��� 6 min read  ·  ��� Time Zones, Guide

In theory Earth has 24 time zones, one per hour of the day. In practice there are 38, because several sit on half-hour or quarter-hour offsets like India (+5:30) and Nepal (+5:45). And counting countries rather than offsets produces bigger numbers still.

Why Half-Hour Offsets Exist

Offsets like +5:30 (India), +3:30 (Iran), +9:30 (parts of Australia) and +5:45 (Nepal) trace to local solar noon: a single whole-hour grid didn't line up with where midday actually fell for some countries, so they split the difference.
