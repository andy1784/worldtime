Meeting Planner Tips: How to Schedule Across Time Zones (2026)

Scheduling meetings across time zones is one of the biggest challenges for remote teams and international businesses. Here are proven strategies to make it easier.

The Golden Rule: Find the Overlap

Standard business hours (9 AM - 5 PM local) create limited overlap windows:

  • US East Coast + Europe: 9 AM - 12 PM EST (2-5 PM Europe)
  • US West Coast + Europe: Very limited — 6-8 AM PST (3-5 PM CET)
  • US East Coast + Asia: Very limited — evening US = morning Asia
  • Australia + Europe: 8-10 PM Sydney = 10 AM - 12 PM London

Best Practices for Global Meetings

  1. Rotate meeting times: Don't always make the same person wake up early
  2. Use a meeting planner tool: Our meeting planner visualizes overlapping hours
  3. Send calendar invites: Calendar apps auto-convert to each person's time zone
  4. State the time zone: Always include the time zone (e.g., "3 PM EST / 8 PM GMT")
  5. Record meetings: For those who can't attend live
  6. Be DST-aware: Time differences change twice a year for many regions

Worst Times to Schedule Meetings

  • Friday afternoon (any time zone) — people are winding down
  • Monday morning — catching up from the weekend
  • Lunch hours (12-1 PM local for any participant)
  • Outside 8 AM - 9 PM for any participant
  • During known holidays in participants' countries
