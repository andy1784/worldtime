12PM EST to IST — EST-IST Conversion Guide

Eastern Time and India Standard Time are 10 hours 30 minutes apart (EST) — or 9:30 during EDT. The half-hour remainder comes from IST's unusual UTC+5:30 base.

Key Conversions

EST (winter)EDT (summer)IST
8:00 AM8:00 AM18:30 / 17:30
10:00 AM10:00 AM20:30 / 19:30
12:00 PM12:00 PM22:30 / 21:30
2:00 PM2:00 PM00:30 / 23:30

So 12 PM EST = 10:30 PM IST (or 9:30 PM when EDT applies). Noon calls from New York land late at night in Bangalore.

Finding the US–India Overlap

The only shared working window is Indian late afternoon against US early morning:

  • 8:00–9:30 AM EST = 18:30–20:00 IST — evening India
  • Anything past 11 AM ET pushes India past 21:30
