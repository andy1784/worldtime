Time Zone Converter Guide: How to Convert Time Zones (2026)

Converting time zones doesn't have to be confusing. This guide breaks down everything you need to know about converting time between any two cities in the world.

Understanding UTC Offsets

All time zones are measured as offsets from UTC (Coordinated Universal Time):

  • UTC+0: London (winter), Reykjavik, Accra
  • UTC+1: Paris, Berlin, Rome, Lagos
  • UTC+5:30: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore (India)
  • UTC+8: Beijing, Singapore, Perth, Hong Kong
  • UTC+9: Tokyo, Seoul
  • UTC+10: Sydney (summer), Melbourne, Brisbane
  • UTC-5: New York, Toronto, Miami (winter)
  • UTC-8: Los Angeles, Vancouver, San Francisco (winter)

How to Calculate Time Differences

Simple method:

  1. Find the UTC offset for both cities
  2. Subtract the two offsets
  3. Example: Tokyo (UTC+9) to London (UTC+0) = 9 hour difference
  4. Example: New York (UTC-5) to Paris (UTC+1) = 6 hour difference

Daylight Saving Time Complications

DST can change the time difference between two cities by 1 hour depending on the date. Key DST dates:

  • US/Canada: 2nd Sunday March - 1st Sunday November
  • Europe: Last Sunday March - Last Sunday October
  • Australia: 1st Sunday October - 1st Sunday April
  • When one hemisphere springs forward, the other falls back — time differences shift by 2 hours!

Half-Quarter Hour Time Zones

Some time zones use unusual offsets:

  • India: UTC+5:30
  • Nepal: UTC+5:45
  • Australia Central: UTC+9:30
  • Newfoundland: UTC-3:30
  • Iran: UTC+3:30
  • Myanmar: UTC+6:30
