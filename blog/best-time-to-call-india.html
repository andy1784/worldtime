Best Time to Call India from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

India uses IST (Indian Standard Time, UTC+5:30) year-round. The half-hour offset makes it unique among world time zones.

India Time Zone Facts

  • IST (Indian Standard Time): UTC+5:30, no DST
  • India is 5.5 hours ahead of GMT/UTC
  • India is 10.5 hours ahead of US Eastern (EST)

Best Times to Call India from the US

  • 7-9 AM EST = 5:30-7:30 PM IST (perfect for after-work calls)
  • 8-10 PM EST = 6:30-8:30 AM IST next day (good for morning India)

Popular Indian cities: Mumbai | Delhi | Bangalore | Chennai | Kolkata

Best Times to Call India from the UK

  • 12-2 PM GMT = 5:30-7:30 PM IST (ideal overlap)
  • 9-11 AM GMT = 2:30-4:30 PM IST (good for business calls)

India Business Hours & Holidays

  • Standard business hours: 9:30 AM - 6 PM IST
  • IT sector: Often 9 AM - 7 PM IST
  • Major holidays: Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas (offices may close)
