Finding a meeting time that works for London, New York, and Tokyo shouldn't take 20 minutes of mental math. Our meeting time zone converter does it in seconds.

How It Works

  1. Enter up to 10 cities
  2. See a color-coded timeline: green = everyone in work hours, yellow = some outside, red = someone at 3 AM
  3. Drag to the green zone
  4. Copy the UTC + 3-cities line for your calendar invite

Why This Beats Manual Conversion

  • DST-aware: Knows when New York springs forward but London hasn't yet
  • IANA zones: Uses America/New_York, not "EST" (which is ambiguous half the year)
  • Visual: You see the whole day at once, not one conversion at a time
  • Shareable: One link shows the whole team the same view

Example: SF + London + Singapore

Add the three cities. The converter shows:

  • Green zone: 15:00–16:00 UTC (07:00–08:00 SF / 15:00–16:00 London / 23:00–00:00 Singapore)
  • That's the only window where all three are in reasonable hours
  • If Singapore is optional, 13:00–15:00 UTC opens up

Pro Tips

  • Bookmark the result link — it saves your city list
  • Use "business hours" toggle to highlight 09:00–17:00 local for each city
  • For recurring meetings, check the DST transition weeks (March/November) — the green zone shifts
