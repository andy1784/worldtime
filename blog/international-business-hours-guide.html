Why Business Hours Matter

Running a global business means coordinating across time zones. Knowing when offices are open — and when they're closed — can save you from awkward 3 AM calls and missed connections. This guide covers typical business hours in 30+ countries, so you can schedule calls with confidence.

Country-Specific Business Hours

United States & Canada

Standard hours: 9 AM – 5 PM local time, Monday through Friday. Lunch is typically 12–1 PM. Tech companies on the West Coast (PST/PDT) often start later, around 10 AM. Government offices may close earlier at 4:30 PM.

United Kingdom

Standard hours: 9 AM – 5:30 PM GMT/BST. Many offices take a full hour for lunch between 12–1 PM. The City of London financial district tends to have longer hours, often 8 AM – 6 PM.

Germany

Standard hours: 9 AM – 5 PM CET/CEST. Germans value punctuality — calls should start exactly on time. Lunch breaks are typically 30–60 minutes. Many offices close promptly at 5 PM.

Japan

Standard hours: 9 AM – 6 PM JST, but overtime is common. Lunch is 12–1 PM. Japanese business culture values face-to-face meetings, so video calls during business hours are preferred.

India

Standard hours: 9 AM – 5:30 PM IST. Lunch is typically 1–2 PM. India's single time zone (UTC+5:30) means the entire country operates on the same schedule, which simplifies coordination.

UAE & Middle East

Standard hours: 9 AM – 5 PM GST (UTC+4). The work week is Sunday through Thursday. Friday and Saturday are the weekend. Lunch breaks may be longer, especially during Ramadan.

Holidays to Watch Out For

Beyond time zones, national holidays can disrupt business schedules:

  • China: Chinese New Year (late Jan/Feb) — week-long shutdown
  • Japan: Golden Week (late April/early May) — multiple holidays
  • Europe: August is a common vacation month, especially in France and Germany
  • India: Diwali (Oct/Nov) — major holiday, many offices close
  • Middle East: Ramadan — shorter working hours, Eid al-Fitr holiday
  • US: Thanksgiving (4th Thu Nov) — many offices closed Thu–Fri
