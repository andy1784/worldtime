The Unix timestamp counts seconds elapsed since January 1, 1970 00:00 UTC (the "epoch"). It is timezone-free by design, which is why databases and APIs use it everywhere.

Converting By Hand

Timestamp → date: divide by 86,400 for days since 1970; the remainder gives time of day. In practice every language has a one-liner — JavaScript new Date(ts*1000), Python datetime.fromtimestamp(ts, tz=timezone.utc).
