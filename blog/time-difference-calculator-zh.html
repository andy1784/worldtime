如何计算任意两个城市之间的时差

What's the time difference between São Paulo and Jakarta? Between Moscow and Buenos Aires? Between Cairo and Seoul?

You could Google it every time. Or you could learn a quick method that works for any pair of cities.

The Simple Method

Every time zone has a UTC offset. To find the difference between two cities:

  1. Look up the UTC offset for each city
  2. Subtract the two offsets
  3. The result is the time difference

Example: São Paulo (UTC-3) and Jakarta (UTC+7)

Difference = 7 − (−3) = 10 hours. Jakarta is 10 hours ahead of São Paulo.

Example: Moscow (UTC+3) and Buenos Aires (UTC-3)

Difference = 3 − (−3) = 6 hours. Moscow is 6 hours ahead of Buenos Aires.

The Half-Hour Problem

Some cities have 30-minute or 45-minute offsets. India (UTC+5:30), Iran (UTC+3:30), Afghanistan (UTC+4:30), Nepal (UTC+5:45), parts of Australia (UTC+9:30, UTC+10:30), and Newfoundland (UTC-3:30).

When calculating with these zones, you need to account for the extra 30 or 45 minutes. London to India: UTC+0 to UTC+5:30 = 5.5 hours, not 5.

DST Changes the Math

Remember that UTC offsets change when DST is active. The time difference between New York and London is 5 hours for most of the year, but 4 hours during the DST overlap period (late March to late October).

Between New York and Tokyo, the difference is always 13–14 hours (depending on US DST), because Japan doesn't observe DST.

Between London and Sydney, the difference changes 2–4 times per year because both regions observe DST but on different schedules.
