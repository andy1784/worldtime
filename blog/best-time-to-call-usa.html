Best Time to Call the USA from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

Calling the United States from abroad? With 6 time zones spanning from Hawaii to New York, finding the right time to call can be tricky. This guide breaks down the best calling times from every region.

US Time Zones at a Glance

  • Eastern (EST/EDT): UTC-5/-4 — New York, Miami, Washington DC
  • Central (CST/CDT): UTC-6/-5 — Chicago, Dallas, Houston
  • Mountain (MST/MDT): UTC-7/-6 — Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake City
  • Pacific (PST/PDT): UTC-8/-7 — Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle
  • Alaska (AKST/AKDT): UTC-9/-8 — Anchorage
  • Hawaii (HST): UTC-10 — Honolulu

Best Times to Call the US from Europe

Europe is 5-8 hours ahead of the US. The sweet spot is late afternoon in Europe:

  • 2-4 PM London time = 9-11 AM Eastern (perfect for East Coast)
  • 1-3 PM London time = 5-7 AM Pacific (good for early risers on West Coast)

Popular US cities: New York | Los Angeles | Chicago | Miami | Houston

Best Times to Call the US from Asia

Asia is 10-16 hours ahead. Best strategy: call in your evening.

  • 8-10 PM Tokyo time = 7-9 AM Eastern
  • 7-9 PM Beijing time = 7-9 AM Eastern
  • 9-11 PM Mumbai time = 9:30-11:30 AM Eastern

Best Times to Call the US from Australia

  • 6-8 PM Sydney time = 4-6 AM Eastern (not ideal — consider email)
  • Best overlap: 8-10 PM Sydney = 6-8 AM Eastern (limited window)

Popular Australian cities: Sydney | Melbourne | Brisbane

US Business Hours

Standard US business hours are 9 AM - 5 PM local time. For the East Coast, that's 2-10 PM GMT. Government offices typically operate 8 AM - 4:30 PM.

Tips for Cheap Calls to the US

  • WhatsApp, FaceTime Audio, and Zoom are free over WiFi
  • Google Voice offers free calls to US numbers
  • Many international carriers offer US calling packages
  • Weekend and evening rates are often cheaper
