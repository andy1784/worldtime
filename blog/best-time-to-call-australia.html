Best Time to Call Australia from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

Australia spans 3 main time zones and some observe DST while others don't. Here's how to navigate calling Australia from anywhere.

Australia Time Zones

  • AEST (Australian Eastern): UTC+10 / AEDT UTC+11 (DST Oct-Apr)
  • ACST (Australian Central): UTC+9:30 / ACDT UTC+10:30 (DST Oct-Apr)
  • AWST (Australian Western): UTC+8, no DST

Best Times to Call Australia from the US

  • 4-6 PM PST (LA) = 9-11 AM AEST next day (Sydney/Melbourne)
  • Best strategy: Call in your late afternoon/evening for their next morning

Popular Australian cities: Sydney | Melbourne | Brisbane | Perth | Adelaide

Best Times to Call Australia from Europe

  • 9-11 PM London = 7-9 AM AEST next day (winter) / 8-10 AM AEDT (summer)
  • Best: Late evening Europe = Early morning Australia

Best Times to Call Australia from Asia

  • 10 AM - 12 PM Tokyo = 11 AM - 1 PM AEST (great overlap!)
  • 9-11 AM Beijing = 11 AM - 1 PM AEST (excellent for business)

Australia Business Hours

  • Standard: 9 AM - 5 PM local time
  • Government: 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM
  • Major holidays: Australia Day (Jan 26), ANZAC Day (Apr 25), Christmas, Boxing Day
