The October Mismatch Week

Europe falls back on October 25, 2026, but North America not until November 1. For one week the usual gaps shift by an hour: London–New York goes from 5 to 4 hours, Paris–San Francisco from 9 to 8. Meetings booked in absolute local times still work; meetings pinned to UTC need re-checking.

What Breaks During Transitions

  • Recurring calendar events pinned to UTC drift an hour for a week.
  • Overnight shifts and flight times published in local time can double-book.
  • Cron jobs and server logs show "duplicate" or "missing" hours.
