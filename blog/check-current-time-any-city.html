How to Check the Current Time in Any City

📅 October 10, 2026  ·  ⏱ 6 min read  ·  🏷 Time Zones, Tools, Tutorial

You need to know what time it is in Sydney right now — maybe for a call, a flight, or just curiosity. You do not need to install an app or remember UTC offsets. Here are the fastest ways to get an accurate answer in seconds.

1. Type It in Your Browser Address Bar

Most modern browsers answer time queries directly in the address bar. Just type:

time in tokyo

or

what time is it in london

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari all show the current local time above the search results. No click needed. This works for cities, time zone abbreviations (time in est), and even coordinates.

2. Use a Dedicated World Clock Page

When you need several cities at once, a world clock page beats repeated searches. Open our world clock, pin the cities you care about, and they update every second. You get:

  • Live time for 700+ cities
  • Day/night indicators
  • UTC offsets and DST status
  • Shareable links with your city set pre-selected

Bookmark it once and you never search for "current time in" again.

3. Voice Assistants (Phone, Watch, Smart Speaker)

Siri, Google Assistant, and Alexa all answer "What time is it in [city]?" instantly. This is fastest when your hands are busy — cooking, driving, or already on a call.

4. Command Line (For Developers)

If you live in a terminal, curl wttr.in/london or curl wttr.in/tokyo returns a compact time card. The date command with TZ works too:

TZ=America/New_York date

No API keys, no rate limits for reasonable use.

5. Convert Between Your Time and Theirs

Knowing "it's 14:00 in Tokyo" is only half the answer — you usually need "what is that for me?" Use our time zone converter to translate any time between any two cities, with automatic DST handling. Enter your city, their city, and the time — get the match instantly.

Quick Reference: The Three Questions

  • One city, right now: browser address bar or voice assistant
  • Multiple cities, ongoing: pinned world clock page
  • Your time vs. their time: time zone converter

All three are free, work on any device, and need zero setup. Next time someone asks "what time is it there?", you will have the answer before they finish the question.
