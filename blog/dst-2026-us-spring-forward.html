Daylight Saving Time 2026: US Spring Forward (Spring forward) - Complete Guide

📅 February 22, 2026  ·  ⏱ 7 min read  ·  🏷 Time Zones, DST, Guide

When Does the Time Change?

Date: March 08, 2026 (Sunday)

Change: Clocks move spring forward by +1 hour at 2:00 AM local time.

Affected Countries: United States, Canada (most), Mexico (border)

Affected Time Zones

IANA Time ZoneChange

Impact on International Meetings

  • Meeting Planner: The offset between affected zones and non-affected zones shifts by 1 hour for ~1-2 weeks. Use our Meeting Planner to verify overlap.
  • Cross-border calls: If your counterpart is in a zone that doesn't change (e.g., Arizona, Japan, India), your meeting time relative to them shifts.
  • Recurring meetings: Calendar apps usually handle this, but verify recurring series around the transition.

Quick Reference: Major City Offsets After Change

  • New York: UTC-4 (EDT)
  • London: UTC+1 (BST)
  • Paris: UTC+2 (CEST)
  • Tokyo: UTC+9 (JST, no DST)
  • Sydney: UTC+11 (AEDT) / UTC+10 (AEST)

What You Need to Do

  1. Update any hardcoded UTC offsets in your code/config
  2. Verify recurring meetings in the week of the change
  3. Check that your servers/tzdata are updated (run tzdata update)
  4. Use Meeting Planner for cross-zone scheduling
