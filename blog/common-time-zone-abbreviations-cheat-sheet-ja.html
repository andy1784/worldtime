Remember

  • Abbreviations like CST, IST, and MST are ambiguous. Use IANA names for precision.
  • DST changes the UTC offset but not the IANA name. America/New_York is always correct, whether it's EST or EDT.
  • Half-hour offsets: India (UTC+5:30), Iran (UTC+3:30), Afghanistan (UTC+4:30), Nepal (UTC+5:45), parts of Australia (UTC+9:30, UTC+10:30), Newfoundland (UTC-3:30).
  • UTC never changes. It's always the same. Use it as your reference point.
