Time Zone Considerations During Ramadan

  • DST transitions: Some countries (e.g., US, EU) change clocks during Ramadan 2026 — adjust schedules accordingly.
  • Long fasts at high latitudes: Cities like Reykjavik, Oslo, and Stockholm may have 20+ hour fasts. Fatwas allow following Mecca times.
