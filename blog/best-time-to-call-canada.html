Best Time to Call Canada from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

Canada spans 6 time zones, but most business activity is in the Eastern and Pacific zones. Here's how to call Canada from anywhere.

Canada Time Zones

  • EST/EDT (Eastern): UTC-5/-4 — Toronto, Montreal, Ottawa
  • CST/CDT (Central): UTC-6/-5 — Winnipeg, Regina
  • MST/MDT (Mountain): UTC-7/-6 — Calgary, Edmonton
  • PST/PDT (Pacific): UTC-8/-7 — Vancouver
  • NST (Newfoundland): UTC-3:30/-2:30 — St. John's

Best Times to Call Canada from Europe

  • 2-5 PM London = 9 AM - 12 PM EST (perfect for Toronto)
  • 3-6 PM London = 7-10 AM PST (good for Vancouver)

Popular Canadian cities: Toronto | Vancouver | Montreal | Calgary | Ottawa

Best Times to Call Canada from Asia

  • 8-10 PM Tokyo = 7-9 AM EST (good for Toronto morning)
  • 7-9 PM Beijing = 7-9 AM EST

Canada Business Hours

  • Standard: 9 AM - 5 PM local time
  • Major holidays: Canada Day (July 1), Thanksgiving (2nd Mon Oct)
  • Quebec: Different holiday schedule (Saint-Jean-Baptiste Day, June 24)
