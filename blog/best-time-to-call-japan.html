Best Time to Call Japan from Abroad: Complete Guide for 2026

Japan uses JST (Japan Standard Time, UTC+9) year-round with no daylight saving time. This makes calculations easier but the time gap with the West is significant.

Japan Time Zone Facts

  • JST (Japan Standard Time): UTC+9, no DST
  • Japan is 9 hours ahead of GMT/UTC
  • Japan is 14 hours ahead of US Eastern (EST)
  • Japan is 17 hours ahead of US Pacific (PST)

Best Times to Call Japan from the US

  • 5-7 PM PST (LA) = 9-11 AM JST next day (perfect for Tokyo business hours)
  • 7-9 PM EST (NYC) = 9-11 AM JST next day
  • Best strategy: Call in your evening for their next-morning

Popular Japanese cities: Tokyo | Osaka | Kyoto | Yokohama

Best Times to Call Japan from Europe

  • 8-10 AM London = 5-7 PM JST (good for end of Japan business day)
  • 9-11 AM CET = 5-7 PM JST

Japan Business Culture Tips

  • Business hours: 9 AM - 5 PM JST (some offices until 7 PM)
  • Lunch: 12-1 PM (avoid calling during lunch)
  • Golden Week (late April/May): Many businesses closed
  • New Year (Dec 28 - Jan 3): Most offices closed
  • Japanese business culture values punctuality — be on time for calls
