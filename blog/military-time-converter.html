Military time uses a 24-hour clock: hours run from 00 (midnight) to 23 (11 PM), and no AM/PM marker is needed. It removes ambiguity in aviation, medicine, logistics and anything else where mixing up morning and evening matters.

Zulu Time Explained

Zulu time (the "Z" suffix, e.g. 1430Z) is simply UTC. Pilots file flight plans and log departures in Zulu so schedules never shift across zones. To read Zulu locally: take UTC and apply your own offset.
