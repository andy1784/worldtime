リモートチームミーティングスケジューラー：公正なミーティング時間を自動化

Manual time zone math is a waste of engineering talent. The best remote teams automate meeting scheduling so fair times happen by default.

What a Good Scheduler Does

  1. Imports team members' cities/time zones
  2. Calculates working-hours overlap automatically
  3. Flags DST transition weeks
  4. Generates calendar invites with UTC + 3 local times
  5. Supports rotation rules (e.g., "flip every sprint")

Our Free Toolchain

  • Meeting Planner — visual overlap finder, one-click invite copy
  • Time Difference Calculator — instant two-city conversion
  • World Time Map — see day/night and business hours globally

Workflow: 60 Seconds to a Fair Invite

  1. Open Meeting Planner
  2. Add attendee cities (auto-complete with IANA zones)
  3. Green bar = everyone 08:00–18:00 local
  4. Drag to green, click "Copy Invite"
  5. Paste into Google Calendar / Outlook

Advanced: Recurring with Rotation

For standing meetings, create a recurring event with the rotation in the description:

"Team Sync — Week A: 14:00 UTC (06:00 LA / 14:00 London / 22:00 Singapore)
Week B: 22:00 UTC (14:00 LA / 22:00 London / 06:00 Singapore)
Rotation flips every 2 weeks."

Everyone sees the pattern. No surprises.

When Automation Isn't Enough

If your team spans >12 hours (Americas + Asia), no tool finds a "good" window. The answer isn't better software — it's async-first culture. Replace the recurring call with a weekly written update + 15-min optional live chat. Teams that do this ship faster because they protect focus time.
