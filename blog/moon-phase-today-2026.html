How the Cycle Works

A synodic month runs 29 days, 12 hours and 44 minutes. Half the cycle the Moon waxes (grows from new to full), half it wanes. Because the calendar month averages 30.4 days, some months fit two full moons — the second one is called a blue moon. May 2026 is one of those months.
