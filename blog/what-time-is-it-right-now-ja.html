It's the most basic question in the world: "What time is it?" But when you're working with people in different countries, the answer isn't so simple. It's 3 PM where you are, but what time is it where they are?

The Quick Answer

Our homepage shows live clocks for cities worldwide. Just search for any city and you'll see the current local time, updated every second.

But if you want to understand time zones more deeply — not just check the current time — here's what you need to know.

Why "What Time Is It?" Is Complicated

There are 24 primary time zones, but the actual number of distinct time zones is closer to 38 when you account for half-hour and 45-minute offsets. Some countries observe DST, some don't. Some observe it on different dates. And time zone boundaries don't follow neat lines of longitude — they follow political borders.

This means the time difference between two cities can change during the year. New York and London are 5 hours apart for most of the year, but 4 hours apart during the DST overlap period.

Current Time in Major Cities

Here are the time zones for major world cities (standard time):

  • New York — EST (UTC-5) / EDT (UTC-4)
  • London — GMT (UTC+0) / BST (UTC+1)
  • Paris — CET (UTC+1) / CEST (UTC+2)
  • Dubai — GST (UTC+4)
  • Mumbai — IST (UTC+5:30)
  • Bangkok — ICT (UTC+7)
  • Singapore — SGT (UTC+8)
  • Shanghai — CST (UTC+8)
  • Tokyo — JST (UTC+9)
  • Sydney — AEST (UTC+10) / AEDT (UTC+11)
  • Los Angeles — PST (UTC-8) / PDT (UTC-7)

How to Check the Time Anywhere

  1. Use our world clock — search any city, see the current time
  2. Google it — "time in [city name]" gives an instant answer
  3. Check your phone — most phones have a world clock widget
  4. Use IANA names — for technical applications, use names like "America/New_York" which handle DST automatically

Pro Tip

If you frequently check the time in the same cities, add them to your phone's world clock. On iPhone: Clock app → World Clock → +. On Android: Clock app → World Clock → Add city. This gives you instant access without searching.
