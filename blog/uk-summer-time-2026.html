UK Summer Time 2026 — BST Dates & Clock Changes

The UK follows British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1) from late March to late October, then returns to GMT (UTC+0). In 2026 the switch dates are:

UK Clock Changes 2026

ChangeDateEffect
Clocks go forwardSunday, March 29 at 01:00 GMTGMT → BST (+1)
Clocks go backSunday, October 25 at 02:00 BSTBST → GMT (−1)

Both switches happen at 01:00 UTC — the same moment as the rest of the EU, which keeps European time gaps stable all year.

What Stays Constant vs What Shifts

  • London–New York: normally 5 hours apart; becomes 4 hours for the week between Oct 25 and Nov 1 (Europe falls back first).
  • London–India: 5:30 in winter, 4:30 in summer.
  • London–Sydney: swings between 9–11 hours because both countries observe DST in opposite seasons.
