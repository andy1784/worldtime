World Clock for Remote Teams: Best Tools & Setup for 2026

Managing a distributed team across time zones? A reliable world clock is essential. Here's how to set up the perfect time zone workflow for your remote team.

Why Remote Teams Need a World Clock

  • Avoid calling teammates at 3 AM
  • Schedule meetings during overlapping business hours
  • Set clear expectations for response times
  • Plan sprint deadlines across time zones
  • Respect local holidays and work schedules

Best World Clock Setups

Browser-Based

Our World Time Sync homepage shows live clocks for 700+ cities. Bookmark it and share with your team.

Desktop Widgets

  • Windows: Clock app supports multiple time zones
  • macOS: World Clock widget in Notification Center
  • Linux: GNOME Clocks or Conky

Slack/Teams Integration

  • Use /time [city] bots in Slack
  • Set your time zone in Slack profile
  • Microsoft Teams shows colleague time zones

Meeting Planner for Teams

Our meeting planner tool lets you add multiple cities and instantly see overlapping business hours. Perfect for finding the best time for your global standup.

Pro Tips for Distributed Teams

  1. Create a shared "team time zone" document
  2. Use UTC for all internal deadlines
  3. Rotate meeting times so no one is always inconvenienced
  4. Record all meetings for those who can't attend
  5. Set "core hours" where everyone overlaps (even if just 2-3 hours)
  6. Be aware of DST changes — they affect different countries at different times
