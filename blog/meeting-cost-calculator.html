Every recurring call with several salaried attendees burns real money every week. A daily 15-minute standup with 8 people earning $80k each costs roughly $46,000 per year. Here's the math and a quick calculator.

Where the Cost Hides in Distributed Teams

  • Inconvenient hours — a 9 PM call costs more than the same hour at noon: fatigue tax on top of salary.
  • Ramp-up time — people join 2–3 minutes early; that's part of the bill.
  • Context switching — a 30-min call can eat an hour of focus work around it.

Cutting the Bill Without Cutting Alignment

  1. Shorten default invites to 25 or 50 minutes.
  2. Replace status meetings with written updates.
  3. Schedule inside the overlap window only — use the overlap guide.
  4. Rotate who takes the painful hour so no region always pays it.
