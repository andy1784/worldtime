Лучшее время для звонка в Индию: практическое руководство на 2026 год

Here's something that trips up almost everyone who needs to call India for the first time: the half-hour offset. India runs on IST (Indian Standard Time), which is UTC+5:30 — not a clean +5 or +6. That 30-minute difference means your usual mental math for time zones doesn't quite work.

India doesn't observe daylight saving time, which actually makes things easier. The offset stays the same year-round. No spring-forward, fall-back confusion.

IST at a Glance

  • Time zone: IST (Indian Standard Time), UTC+5:30
  • DST: None — stays the same all year
  • Major cities on IST: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad
  • Population on this time zone: ~1.4 billion people

Calling India from the US

The time difference between the US and India ranges from 9.5 to 12.5 hours depending on which US time zone you're in and whether DST is active. That's a wide window, so let's break it down:

Your Time (US)India TimeGood For
7:00–9:00 AM EST5:30–7:30 PM ISTAfter-work calls, catching people before dinner
8:00–10:00 PM EST6:30–8:30 AM IST (next day)Morning calls, start of business day
10:00 PM–12:00 AM EST8:30–10:30 AM IST (next day)Mid-morning, good for scheduled meetings

From Pacific time, shift all of these back 3 hours. So 4:00–6:00 AM PST = 5:30–7:30 PM IST.

Calling India from the UK

The UK-India gap is 4.5 hours (GMT) or 5.5 hours (BST during summer). The overlap is tight:

  • 12:00–2:00 PM GMT = 5:30–7:30 PM IST — this is your sweet spot
  • 9:00–11:00 AM BST = 2:30–4:30 PM IST — works for afternoon business calls

Outside those windows, someone is going to be annoyed. Calling an Indian colleague at 11 PM their time because it's 5:30 PM in London is a quick way to burn bridges.

Calling from Australia

Sydney (AEST, UTC+10) is 4.5 hours ahead of India. Perth (AWST, UTC+8) is 2.5 hours ahead. This actually works well — a 9 AM meeting in Sydney is 4:30 AM in India (too early), but a 2 PM Sydney meeting is 9:30 AM in India (perfect).

India Business Hours: What to Know

Standard office hours in India are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Saturday in many companies (though Saturday work is becoming less common in tech). The IT sector often runs later — 9 AM to 7 PM isn't unusual.

Lunch is typically 1:00–2:00 PM IST. Don't schedule calls during that hour unless you want to talk to someone's voicemail.

India has a lot of public holidays — Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, Pongal, and many regional festivals. Government offices and many private companies close. If you're scheduling recurring calls, it's worth checking an Indian holiday calendar.

Common Mistakes

Forgetting the half-hour: India is UTC+5:30, not UTC+5. If you calculate based on UTC+5, you'll be 30 minutes off. This matters when you're trying to catch someone right at the end of their workday.

Assuming all of India is the same: It is — India uses a single time zone despite spanning what would naturally be two. But business culture varies significantly between cities. Bangalore's tech companies may have flexible hours; Mumbai's financial sector runs on a more traditional schedule.

Calling during cricket matches: This isn't a joke. If India is playing a major cricket match, especially against Pakistan or Australia, a significant portion of the country is not available. Check the cricket calendar.
