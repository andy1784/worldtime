The confusing ones

CST is the worst offender: North American Central Standard Time (UTC−6) versus China Standard Time (UTC+8). IST triples up — India, Ireland and Israel all claim it. CET occasionally collides with Cuba Standard Time in older documents. When precision matters, prefer explicit offsets or IANA names like America/New_York.

Note on military time: the NATO phonetic letter Z ("Zulu") denotes UTC+0. Pilots and militaries say "1200 Zulu" instead of "noon UTC" to avoid any ambiguity.

Quick answers

What does EST stand for?

Eastern Standard Time, UTC−5 — the US East Coast and eastern Canada.

Is GMT the same as UTC?

Practically yes, both are UTC+0. GMT is the historical astronomical standard; UTC is maintained by atomic clocks.

Why does CST mean two different things?

North America uses CST for UTC−6; Asia often uses it for China Standard Time, UTC+8.

What is Zulu time?

Zulu (Z) is military/aviation shorthand for UTC+0. “1200 Zulu” means noon UTC.
