PST to CET Converter — Chart for Meetings & Calls

August 10, 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  Time Zones, Guide

Pacific Time and Central European Time sit nine hours apart most of the year (PST→CET = +9, PDT→CEST = +9). Because both regions switch to daylight saving — on different dates — the gap briefly shrinks or stretches by an hour in March and late October.
