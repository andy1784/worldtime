Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

We'll keep this short. We built World Time Sync to be useful, not to harvest your data.

What We Collect

When you visit our site, we use:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage data (pages visited, approximate location by country, device type). This helps us understand which features people use so we can improve them.
  • Google AdSense — shows ads. Google may use cookies to personalize ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized ads through Google's ad settings.

We don't require registration. We don't collect email addresses (unless you contact us). We don't sell data to anyone.

What We Don't Collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No personal information unless you email us
  • No tracking beyond standard analytics
  • No data sold to third parties
  • No cookies beyond what Google services set

Contact Form

If you use our contact form, your message is sent to us via email. We use it to respond to you and then delete it. We don't store contact form submissions in a database.

Third-Party Links

Our site links to external resources (city pages, blog posts, etc.). We're not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites we link to.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We won't make dramatic changes — we don't have the infrastructure to misuse your data even if we wanted to.

Questions?

Email us at support@worldtimessync.com. We read every message.
