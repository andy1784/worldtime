Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

We'll keep this short. We built World Time Sync to be useful, not to harvest your data.

What We Collect

When you visit our site, we use:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage data (pages visited, approximate location by country, device type). This helps us understand which features people use so we can improve them.
  • Google AdSense — shows ads. Google may use cookies to personalize ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized ads through Google's ad settings.

We don't require registration. We don't collect email addresses (unless you contact us). We don't sell data to anyone.

What We Don't Collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No personal information unless you email us
  • No tracking beyond standard analytics
  • No data sold to third parties
  • No cookies beyond what Google services set

Contact Form

If you use our contact form, your message is sent to us via email. We use it to respond to you and then delete it. We don't store contact form submissions in a database.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies to distinguish unique users and throttle request rates. Google AdSense may set cookies to personalize ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings — our site will still function correctly, though analytics data will be incomplete.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — advertising. AdSense Terms
  • Vercel — hosting and CDN. Vercel Privacy Policy

Third-Party Links

Our site links to external resources (city pages, blog posts, etc.). We're not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites we link to.

Data Retention

We don't maintain a user database. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics settings (26 months by default). Contact form submissions are read, responded to, and then deleted. We don't store form data on our servers.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about what data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of any personal data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking (use a browser extension or Google's opt-out)
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via Google's ad settings

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We won't make dramatic changes — we don't have the infrastructure to misuse your data even if we wanted to.

Questions?

Email us at support@worldtimessync.com. We read every message.
