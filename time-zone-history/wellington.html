Frequently Asked Questions

When did Wellington last change its time zone?

Wellington follows the Pacific/Auckland time zone. The most recent base-offset change is shown in the table above; the city currently observes UTC+13:00.

Does Wellington observe daylight saving time?

Yes, Wellington currently observes daylight saving time (NZDT). Clocks move forward by 1 hour in spring and back by 1 hour in autumn.

What is the current UTC offset for Wellington?

Wellington is on UTC+13:00 right now (time zone: Pacific/Auckland).
