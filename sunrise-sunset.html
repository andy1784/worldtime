What the Tool Shows

  • Sunrise time — when the top edge of the Sun first appears above the horizon.
  • Sunset time — when the Sun fully disappears below the horizon in the evening.
  • Daylight duration — the total length of the day between sunrise and sunset.
  • Golden hour — the soft-light window after sunrise and before sunset, ideal for photography.
  • Civil twilight — the period of usable natural light before sunrise and after sunset.

Why Sunrise & Sunset Times Vary

Sunrise and sunset are not fixed — they shift every day because of Earth's 23.5° axial tilt. Near the equinoxes (March and September) day and night are roughly equal everywhere. Near the summer solstice, higher latitudes enjoy very long days; near the winter solstice, the opposite. Our calculator accounts for your location's latitude and the date you select.

Daylight Saving and Your Local Time

If your region observes daylight saving time, the clock times of sunrise and sunset shift by an hour when the clocks change. The calculator automatically applies the correct rules for the date and city you choose, so you always see local civil time — not UTC.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the sunrise time for my city?

Use our Sunrise Sunset calculator above. Enter your city or pick it from the list, and you'll see today's sunrise and sunset times, plus daylight duration. Times adjust automatically for your selected date and daylight saving rules.

What is the golden hour?

Golden hour is the period shortly after sunrise or before sunset when sunlight is softer and warmer. It typically lasts about 60 minutes and is favoured by photographers. Our tool shows the approximate golden hour window for any location.

Why do sunrise times change through the year?

Earth's axial tilt means the Sun's path across the sky shifts with the seasons. Around the summer solstice, days are longest and sunrise is earliest; around the winter solstice, days are shortest and sunrise is latest. The calculator reflects these changes for any date you choose.

What is civil twilight?

Civil twilight is the time after sunset (or before sunrise) when there is still enough natural light for most outdoor activities without artificial lighting. It ends when the Sun is about 6 degrees below the horizon.
