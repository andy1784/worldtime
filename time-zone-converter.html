Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert time between two time zones?

Select two cities in the converter above. It shows the exact time difference in hours and minutes, current local times in both cities, and overlapping business hours (9 AM–5 PM) for scheduling meetings.

Does this time zone converter account for daylight saving time?

Yes — the converter uses the IANA time zone database which automatically applies all current DST rules. It shows the real-time offset including any active daylight saving adjustments.

How do I find the best meeting time for international teams?

The converter shows overlapping business hours automatically. For complex multi-city meetings, use our Meeting Planner which visualizes overlap across 10+ cities simultaneously.

What about half-hour time zones like India or Nepal?

Fully supported — zones like India (UTC+5:30), Nepal (UTC+5:45), Iran (UTC+3:30), Afghanistan (UTC+4:30), and Australia's Northern Territory (UTC+9:30) are all handled correctly.

Can I convert a specific time (not just 'now')?

This page shows the current time difference. For scheduling a specific future date/time, use our Meeting Planner which lets you pick any date and see the converted times.
