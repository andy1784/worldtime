Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert time between two time zones?

Use the converter above: select the source city/time zone, target city/time zone, enter the time to convert, and click Convert. The result shows instantly with automatic DST adjustments.

Does this time zone converter handle daylight saving time?

Yes, all conversions automatically account for Daylight Saving Time (DST) transitions in both source and target time zones. The converter uses the IANA time zone database for accuracy.

Can I convert time for a specific date (not just now)?

The converter uses today's date by default. For historical or future dates, use our Meeting Planner which supports date-specific conversions across multiple cities.

What cities and time zones are supported?

700+ cities worldwide covering all IANA time zones. Search by city name (e.g., New York, London, Tokyo) or time zone abbreviation (EST, PST, CET, JST, IST).

Is this time zone converter free?

Yes, completely free. No registration required. Use it as often as you need for personal or business purposes.
