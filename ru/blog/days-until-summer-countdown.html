Why the Solstice Moves

The solstice drifts between June 20–22 because the calendar year (365 days) doesn't match the tropical year (~365.24 days) — leap years pull it back into step. The same wobble shifts sunrise and sunset times each year; check exact times per city via sunrise & sunset.
