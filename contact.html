Frequently Asked Questions

Is World Time Sync free?

Yes! World Time Sync is completely free to use. No registration, no subscription, no hidden fees.

How accurate is the time?

We use the IANA Time Zone Database and NTP servers for maximum accuracy. Our times are typically accurate within 1 second of atomic clock time.

Do I need to install anything?

No. World Time Sync works entirely in your web browser. No downloads, no plugins, no extensions needed.

Which cities are supported?

We currently support over 700 cities across all continents, covering all major capitals, business hubs, and popular destinations.

How often is the time updated?

The displayed time updates every second in real-time, synchronized with NTP servers.

Response Time

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours. During peak times, it may take up to 72 hours.
