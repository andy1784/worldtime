About World Time Online

Welcome to World Time Online, your trusted source for accurate current time information for cities worldwide. Since our launch, we've been dedicated to helping individuals and businesses stay connected across different time zones.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to provide free, accurate, and reliable time information for any city in the world. We understand that in today's globalized society, knowing the correct local time is essential for international communication, business operations, travel planning, and maintaining personal connections across borders.

What We Offer

  • Real-Time Clock: Live updates showing the exact current time in over 400 cities worldwide
  • Timezone Information: Detailed timezone data including UTC offsets and daylight saving time status
  • Global Coverage: Cities from all continents and major time zones
  • Mobile-Friendly: Access our service from any device, anywhere
  • Free Service: No registration or payment required

Why Choose Us?

World Time Online stands out for its accuracy, reliability, and ease of use. Our time data is sourced from authoritative timezone databases and updated regularly to account for changes in daylight saving time and timezone regulations.

We serve millions of users including:

  • Business professionals scheduling international meetings
  • Travelers planning trips across time zones
  • Remote workers coordinating with global teams
  • Families staying connected with loved ones abroad
  • Students and researchers working on international projects

Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of accuracy and reliability. Our team continuously monitors timezone changes and updates our database to ensure you always get the correct time information.

Privacy is also important to us. We collect minimal data and never sell your personal information to third parties. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions, suggestions, or notice any inaccuracies, please don't hesitate to contact us. We value your feedback and strive to continuously improve our service.

Thank you for choosing World Time Online as your trusted time reference!
