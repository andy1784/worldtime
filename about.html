About World Time Online

What is World Time Online?

World Time Online is a free, real-time world clock application that helps you check the current local time in over 700 cities across all time zones worldwide. Whether you're coordinating with colleagues in different countries, planning international meetings, or simply curious about the current time in distant cities, World Time Online provides instant, accurate information.

How It Works

World Time Online uses JavaScript to fetch the current time from your device and calculate the corresponding time in each city based on UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) and the city's time zone offset. The application maintains a comprehensive database of cities, their time zones, and daylight saving time rules to ensure accuracy.

Understanding Time Zones

Time zones are regions of the Earth that observe a uniform standard time for legal, commercial, and social purposes. The world is divided into 24 time zones, each roughly 15 degrees of longitude wide. The reference point is UTC (Coordinated Universal Time), also known as GMT (Greenwich Mean Time). Cities east of the Prime Meridian are ahead of UTC, while cities west are behind.

Daylight Saving Time (DST)

Many countries and regions observe Daylight Saving Time (DST), a practice of advancing clocks by one hour during warmer months to make better use of daylight. World Time Online automatically accounts for DST transitions, ensuring that the times displayed are always accurate. In the United States, DST typically runs from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November. Different countries have different DST schedules, and some do not observe DST at all.
