About Hawaii Standard Time (HST)

Hawaii Standard Time (HST) is a time zone in Pacific (Hawaii) with offset UTC-10. It serves approximately ~1 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-10
  • DST offset: N/A (no DST)
  • Daylight Saving: No — this time zone stays at UTC-10 year-round.
  • IANA zones: Pacific/Honolulu
