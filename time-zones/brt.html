About Brasilia Time (BRT)

Brasilia Time (BRT) is a time zone in Eastern South America with offset UTC-3. It serves approximately ~150 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-3
  • DST offset: N/A (no DST)
  • Daylight Saving: No — this time zone stays at UTC-3 year-round.
  • IANA zones: America/Sao_Paulo, America/Rio_Branco, America/Buenos_Aires
