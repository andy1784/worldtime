About Mountain Standard Time (MST)

Mountain Standard Time (MST) is a time zone in North America with offset UTC-7. It serves approximately ~25 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-7
  • DST offset: UTC-6
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — MDT (UTC-6) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: America/Denver, America/Phoenix, America/Edmonton

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mountain Standard Time (MST)?

Mountain Standard Time (MST) is a time zone with offset UTC-7 covering North America.

Does MST observe daylight saving time?

Yes, Mountain Standard Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to MDT (UTC-6) during summer months.

What cities are in MST?

Major cities include Albuquerque, Calgary, Denver, Edmonton, Mesa, Phoenix, Salt Lake City, Tucson, and more.
