About Eastern Standard Time (EST)

Eastern Standard Time (EST) is a time zone in North America with offset UTC-5. It serves approximately ~120 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-5
  • DST offset: UTC-4
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — EDT (UTC-4) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: America/New_York, America/Toronto, America/Montreal

What is Eastern Standard Time (EST)?

Eastern Standard Time (EST) is a time zone with offset UTC-5 covering North America.

Yes, Eastern Standard Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to EDT (UTC-4) during summer months.
