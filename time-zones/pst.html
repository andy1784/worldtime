About Pacific Standard Time (PST)

Pacific Standard Time (PST) is a time zone in North America with offset UTC-8. It serves approximately ~50 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-8
  • DST offset: UTC-7
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — PDT (UTC-7) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: America/Los_Angeles, America/Vancouver, America/Tijuana

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pacific Standard Time (PST)?

Pacific Standard Time (PST) is a time zone with offset UTC-8 covering North America.

Does PST observe daylight saving time?

Yes, Pacific Standard Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to PDT (UTC-7) during summer months.

What cities are in PST?

Major cities include Fresno, Kelowna, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Oakland, Portland, Sacramento, San Diego, and more.
