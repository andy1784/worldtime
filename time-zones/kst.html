About Korea Standard Time (KST)

Korea Standard Time (KST) is a time zone in Korean Peninsula with offset UTC+9. It serves approximately ~80 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+9
  • DST offset: N/A (no DST)
  • Daylight Saving: No — this time zone stays at UTC+9 year-round.
  • IANA zones: Asia/Seoul, Asia/Pyongyang

What is Korea Standard Time (KST)?

Korea Standard Time (KST) is a time zone with offset UTC+9 covering Korean Peninsula.

No, Korea Standard Time does not observe daylight saving time. The offset stays at UTC+9 all year.
