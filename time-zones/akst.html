About Alaska Standard Time (AKST)

Alaska Standard Time (AKST) is a time zone in North America (Alaska) with offset UTC-9. It serves approximately ~730,000 people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-9
  • DST offset: UTC-8
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — AKDT (UTC-8) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: America/Anchorage, America/Juneau
