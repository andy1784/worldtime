About India Standard Time (IST)

India Standard Time (IST) is a time zone in South Asia (India, Sri Lanka) with offset UTC+5:30. It serves approximately ~1.4 billion people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+5:30
  • DST offset: N/A (no DST)
  • Daylight Saving: No — this time zone stays at UTC+5:30 year-round.
  • IANA zones: Asia/Kolkata, Asia/Colombo
