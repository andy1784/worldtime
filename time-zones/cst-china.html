About China Standard Time (CST-CHINA)

China Standard Time (CST-CHINA) is a time zone in East Asia with offset UTC+8. It serves approximately ~1.5 billion people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+8
  • DST offset: N/A (no DST)
  • Daylight Saving: No — this time zone stays at UTC+8 year-round.
  • IANA zones: Asia/Shanghai, Asia/Hong_Kong, Asia/Taipei, Asia/Singapore
