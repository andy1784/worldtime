Frequently Asked Questions

How many time zones are there?

There are 38 time zones in use worldwide (including UTC). This page covers the 17 most common zones used by businesses and travelers.

What is UTC?

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is the primary time standard by which the world regulates clocks. It replaced Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) as the reference in 1972.

Why do time zones observe DST?

Daylight saving time (DST) moves clocks forward in summer to extend evening daylight. Not all countries observe it — equatorial regions typically don't change clocks.
