About Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST)

Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST) is a time zone in Australia (East Coast) with offset UTC+10. It serves approximately ~15 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+10
  • DST offset: UTC+11
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — AEDT (UTC+11) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: Australia/Sydney, Australia/Melbourne, Australia/Brisbane

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST)?

Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST) is a time zone with offset UTC+10 covering Australia (East Coast).

Does AEST observe daylight saving time?

Yes, Australian Eastern Standard Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to AEDT (UTC+11) during summer months.
