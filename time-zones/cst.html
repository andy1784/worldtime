About Central Standard Time (CST)

Central Standard Time (CST) is a time zone in North America with offset UTC-6. It serves approximately ~60 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC-6
  • DST offset: UTC-5
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — CDT (UTC-5) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: America/Chicago, America/Mexico_City, America/Winnipeg

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Central Standard Time (CST)?

Central Standard Time (CST) is a time zone with offset UTC-6 covering North America.

Does CST observe daylight saving time?

Yes, Central Standard Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to CDT (UTC-5) during summer months.

What cities are in CST?

Major cities include Acapulco, Austin, Chicago, Dallas, Guadalajara, Houston, Kansas City, Memphis, and more.
