About Eastern European Time (EET)

Eastern European Time (EET) is a time zone in Eastern Europe / Balkans with offset UTC+2. It serves approximately ~120 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+2
  • DST offset: UTC+3
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — EEST (UTC+3) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: Europe/Kiev, Europe/Bucharest, Europe/Athens, Europe/Helsinki

Yes, Eastern European Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to EEST (UTC+3) during summer months.
