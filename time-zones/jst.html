About Japan Standard Time (JST)

Japan Standard Time (JST) is a time zone in Japan with offset UTC+9. It serves approximately ~125 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+9
  • DST offset: N/A (no DST)
  • Daylight Saving: No — this time zone stays at UTC+9 year-round.
  • IANA zones: Asia/Tokyo

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Japan Standard Time (JST)?

Japan Standard Time (JST) is a time zone with offset UTC+9 covering Japan.

Does JST observe daylight saving time?

No, Japan Standard Time does not observe daylight saving time. The offset stays at UTC+9 all year.

What cities are in JST?

Major cities include Chiba, Fukuoka, Hiroshima, Kawasaki, Kitakyushu, Kobe, Kyoto, Nagoya, and more.
