About Central European Time (CET)

Central European Time (CET) is a time zone in Central & Western Europe with offset UTC+1. It serves approximately ~150 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+1
  • DST offset: UTC+2
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — CEST (UTC+2) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: Europe/Berlin, Europe/Paris, Europe/Madrid, Europe/Rome

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Central European Time (CET)?

Central European Time (CET) is a time zone with offset UTC+1 covering Central & Western Europe.

Does CET observe daylight saving time?

Yes, Central European Time observes daylight saving time. Clocks switch to CEST (UTC+2) during summer months.
