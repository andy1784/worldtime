About Greenwich Mean Time (GMT)

Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) is a time zone in Western Europe / West Africa with offset UTC+0. It serves approximately ~70 million people across multiple countries and territories.

UTC Offset & DST

  • Standard offset: UTC+0
  • DST offset: UTC+1
  • Daylight Saving: Yes — BST (UTC+1) from last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • IANA zones: Europe/London, Africa/Accra, Atlantic/Reykjavik
