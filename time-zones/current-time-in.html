Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the time displayed?

Our times are synchronized with atomic clocks via authoritative time servers. The live clock updates every second, providing highly accurate time for your needs.

What countries have the most time zones?

France has the most time zones internationally, covering 12 time zones across its territories including France, French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, and French Polynesia.

How do Daylight Saving Time changes affect the time?

Many countries observe Daylight Saving Time, shifting clocks by one hour during summer months. Our tool automatically accounts for DST when showing current times.

Can I use this for scheduling meetings?

Yes! Use our Meeting Planner which shows overlapping working hours across multiple time zones for easy team coordination.

What is the difference between UTC, GMT, and time zones?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the primary time standard. GMT is used in the UK and Ireland. Time zones are named regions that use the same standard time offset from UTC.
