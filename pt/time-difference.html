Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate time difference?

We use the IANA time zone database with real-time UTC offsets, including daylight switching. The result shows the exact difference right now.

Does this work for half-hour time zones?

Yes — zones like India (UTC+5:30), Nepal (UTC+5:45), and Iran (UTC+3:30) are fully supported.

What are the worst time zones for cross-continental meetings?

PST/JST and HST/Europe pairs have the least overlap. A 9 AM in Tokyo is 5 PM previous-day in New York — nearly impossible for real-time meetings.

Is there a way to find the best meeting time?

Yes — check our Meeting Planner for a visual overlap grid across multiple cities.
