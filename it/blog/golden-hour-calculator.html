Golden Hour Calculator — Blue Hour & Solar Noon Today

August 10, 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  Time Zones, Guide

Photographers and filmmakers plan shoots around the Sun's angle. Golden hour is when the Sun sits within roughly 6° of the horizon; blue hour is the stretch of twilight just after sunset or before sunrise. Both depend on your exact location and date.

How to Read the Numbers

PeriodSun positionLight quality
Golden hour (AM)-6° to +6°Warm, soft, long shadows
Solar noonHighest pointHarsh overhead light
Golden hour (PM)+6° to -0°Warm, directional
Blue hour-4° to -6°Cool, even, diffused

Solar Noon & Day Length

Solar noon is not 12:00 on the clock. It shifts with your position inside your time zone and with daylight saving time. Cities on the eastern edge of a zone see solar noon earlier than cities on the western edge — sometimes by more than half an hour.

Day length changes fastest around the equinoxes (roughly March 20 and September 22), gaining or losing several minutes per day at mid-latitudes. Near the solstices it barely moves for a couple of weeks.

Quick Estimator

Golden hour lasts longer in summer and at higher latitudes. Rule of thumb: duration ≈ 40 minutes at 40° latitude near the equinox, up to an hour or more in summer.
